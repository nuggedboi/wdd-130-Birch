Tips

This page gives you some tips and tricks to using AI. AI has many capabilities, but the more effective you are with the prompts that you give the AI the more you are able to have the AI do.

Tip #1: Always be precise! Telling the AI exactly what you want it to do is important. The less precise you are, the more likely you are to have a result that you are not looking for. For example, if you ask AI to generate ideas for a business but don’t give it the areas that you are interested in, it will give you ideas in all the areas. However, if you narrow it down for the AI, it will be more likely to give you ideas in the specific area that you are interested in.

Tip 2: If you are going to ask it a question in a field you are not familiar with, it helps to have a little bit of knowledge. When you ask the AI a question, you want to be able to understand the response it has. For example, if you have no idea about any computer terms or how computer programming works and you ask it to make you a program, it is going to give you instructions that you have no idea how to follow. So, before you ask a question or when you phrase your question, make sure the answer you receive is an answer that you will understand.

Tip 3: Ask to get your question answered, not to simply ask the question to the AI. You can be the most effective when using AI if you use AI for a purpose. You will be way less effective if you are using AI just for the sake of using AI. If you approach the AI with a specific purpose, you can think of clearer questions and get more precise answers.

Tip 4: AI is a tool, but it is not your friend. While AI can be most effective if you prompt it with well worded prompts, remember that it is still designed to be user-friendly. Don’t spend too much time thinking about it! If the prompt is taking more time than the answer to your question is worth, you are overthinking the prompt. You can still have simple prompts that get your question answered.

Tip 5: Don't overthink it. While AI can be most effective if you prompt it with well worded prompts, remember that it is still designed to be user-friendly. Don’t spend too much time thinking about it! If the prompt is taking more time than the answer to your question is worth, you are overthinking the prompt. You can still have simple prompts that get your question answered.

Tip 6: Don't be afraid of the technology! AI can be dangerous if it is used inappropriately, but so can any technology. Remember that you are the user of the AI and it will only do what you tell it to do. It can’t do anything outside of the chat unless you give it permission too. Many chatbots also have a safety filter that can help you use the technology safely. So, don’t be afraid!
